## English plural inflections

The English plural entries in the `dictionary_entries` table of `assets/quotes/cipherbench.sqlite3` were generated from [AGID 2016.01.19](https://sourceforge.net/projects/wordlist/files/AGID/2016.01.19/) noun inflections and checked against the [ESDB en_US 2026.02.25 size-60 word list](https://github.com/en-wl/wordlist-diff/tree/rel-2026.02.25) and noun lemmas in [Open English WordNet 2025](https://github.com/globalwordnet/english-wordnet/releases/tag/2025-edition). The source selection considers all comma-separated AGID noun alternatives, but rejects uncertain forms (AGID suffix flags or variant level 2 and higher) and forms whose singular is not a WordNet noun lemma. It requires ESDB attestation and includes 2,262 additional regular plurals, 34 additional irregular plurals, and 144 independently attested headwords. Identified abbreviations and codes are removed from the English index across all sources; other irregular plurals and independent headwords stay visible with the regular-plural toggle off. This remains a selective, source-checked list, not a comprehensive English dictionary; the sources attest spellings, inflections, and independent lexical entries, but do not prove every word is common or suitable for every puzzle. The [ESDB copyright and permission notice](licenses/ESDB-Copyright.txt), [AGID README and credits](licenses/AGID-README.txt), and [WordNet attribution and CC BY 4.0 license link](licenses/OEWN-2025-ATTRIBUTION.txt) are bundled as Windows app assets.

## Expanded English vocabulary

The English headword and form entries in `dictionary_entries` extend the dictionary independently of the bundled quotes, using the same pinned ESDB, AGID, and WordNet sources. WordNet lemmas attested by ESDB supply headwords; conservative AGID inflections (only source-attested spellings without uncertain flags or high variant levels, from WordNet-attested stems) supply plural, verb, and comparative/superlative forms. New one- and two-letter headwords/forms are excluded, since those source entries are often letter names or abbreviations rather than useful puzzle words. The English pattern index and Home background share a filtered base list; the index additionally screens abbreviations, derived codes, and Roman numerals from all English input pools while retaining the genuine word `MIX`. `DOS` and `RES` are lower-case attested plurals of *do* and *re*, so they appear only with regular plurals enabled, not as default-visible acronym headwords. WordNet adverbs ending in `-ly` with a matching adjective stem are classified as descriptive forms, not adjectives. Words may have overlapping roles: the unified table stores a row per membership with a numeric `type` (0 base, 1 headword, 2 irregular plural, 3 regular plural, 4 verb, 5 descriptive), retaining source and order. Other original words, independently attested headwords, and irregular plurals stay visible when the optional forms are off. This is a broad, source-checked pool rather than a quote-specific answer list; lexical sources can contain ambiguous entries, so please report questionable words for review.

The original source lists were converted row-for-row into SQLite. There is no quote-entry or custom-word persistence feature.

## English puzzle suggestion frequency

The `english_frequency_scores` table contains offline, centibel-quantized Zipf frequency scores for the English word lists, derived from [wordfreq's English large-list data at commit `912caf64`](https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq/tree/912caf64b657478d1dff1138efdc078947d54bb1). wordfreq combines independent usage corpora including books, news, web text, and subtitles (data through approximately 2021). Scores were derived only from English dictionary sources, **not the bundled quotes**, for directly attested words rather than shipping the full wordfreq database. Words with no score sort last, alphabetically; word frequency never changes word inclusion. This ordering is enabled by default for English puzzle suggestions, can be disabled in Settings to restore the previous order, and does not affect the main Dictionary or Spanish.

The derived score data is distributed under [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). [wordfreq and SUBTLEX attribution](licenses/wordfreq-data-attribution.txt) is bundled as a Windows app asset; wordfreq uses freely available SUBTLEX frequency data created by Marc Brysbaert et al. The pinned `wordfreq/data/large_en.msgpack.gz` source has SHA-256 `dffae8066b78dce0a6667cf5f58e567054f902674667090a7ac8a8a44628b05c`.
