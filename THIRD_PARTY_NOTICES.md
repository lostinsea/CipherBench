## English plural inflections

The English additions in `lib/Shared/words/english_plurals.dart` are generated from [AGID 2016.01.19](https://sourceforge.net/projects/wordlist/files/AGID/2016.01.19/) noun inflections and checked against the [ESDB en_US 2026.02.25 size-60 word list](https://github.com/en-wl/wordlist-diff/tree/rel-2026.02.25) and noun lemmas in [Open English WordNet 2025](https://github.com/globalwordnet/english-wordnet/releases/tag/2025-edition). The generator considers all comma-separated AGID noun alternatives, but rejects uncertain forms (AGID suffix flags or variant level 2 and higher) and forms whose singular is not a WordNet noun lemma. It requires ESDB attestation and produces 2,262 additional regular plurals, 34 additional irregular plurals, and 144 independently attested headwords. Identified abbreviations and codes are removed from the English index across all sources; other irregular plurals and independent headwords stay visible with the regular-plural toggle off. This remains a selective, source-checked list, not a comprehensive English dictionary; the sources attest spellings, inflections, and independent lexical entries, but do not prove every word is common or suitable for every puzzle. The [ESDB copyright and permission notice](licenses/ESDB-Copyright.txt), [AGID README and credits](licenses/AGID-README.txt), and [WordNet attribution and CC BY 4.0 license link](licenses/OEWN-2025-ATTRIBUTION.txt) are bundled as Windows app assets.

Regenerate with `python tool/generate_english_plurals.py <agid-2016.01.19.tar.gz> <en_US.txt> <english-wordnet-2025.xml.gz>` using those exact source versions. The SHA-256 hashes of the source downloads are `15d2d792d309d2dc838bf75d8abcd3feb36708c219dc5158d4fff70b89e601d1`, `4ff7e0b6d86763e1e042ffd746e94cdf4432618702deac303a1669e2a838db04`, and `9ca6d1dcb75f822fdd66617f7d9da48142ace38dd544d6ad5e2feca1674ad3fe`, respectively.

## Expanded English vocabulary

`lib/Shared/words/english_forms.dart` extends the English dictionary independently of the bundled quotes, using the same pinned ESDB, AGID, and WordNet sources. WordNet lemmas attested by ESDB supply headwords; conservative AGID inflections (only source-attested spellings without uncertain flags or high variant levels, from WordNet-attested stems) supply plural, verb, and comparative/superlative forms. New one- and two-letter headwords/forms are excluded, since those source entries are often letter names or abbreviations rather than useful puzzle words. The English pattern index and Home background share a filtered base list; the index additionally screens abbreviations, derived codes, and Roman numerals from all English input pools while retaining the genuine word `MIX`. `DOS` and `RES` are lower-case attested plurals of *do* and *re*, so they appear only with regular plurals enabled, not as default-visible acronym headwords. WordNet adverbs ending in `-ly` with a matching adjective stem are classified as descriptive forms, not adjectives. Words may have overlapping roles. Other original words, independently attested headwords, and irregular plurals stay visible when the optional forms are off. This is a broad, source-checked pool rather than a quote-specific answer list; lexical sources can contain ambiguous entries, so please report questionable words for review.

Regenerate with `python tool/generate_english_forms.py <agid-2016.01.19.tar.gz> <en_US.txt> <english-wordnet-2025.xml.gz>` using the pinned downloads above. It reads no quote files. A future quote-entry feature could separately add reviewed words to a small persistent overlay; this release does not provide quote entry or persistence for custom words.

## English puzzle suggestion frequency

`lib/Shared/words/english_frequency_scores.dart` contains offline, centibel-quantized Zipf frequency scores for the English word lists, derived from [wordfreq's English large-list data at commit `912caf64`](https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq/tree/912caf64b657478d1dff1138efdc078947d54bb1). wordfreq combines independent usage corpora including books, news, web text, and subtitles (data through approximately 2021). The generator reads only the English dictionary source files, **not the bundled quotes**, and embeds scores for directly attested words rather than shipping the full wordfreq database. Words with no score sort last, alphabetically; word frequency never changes word inclusion. This ordering is enabled by default for English puzzle suggestions, can be disabled in Settings to restore the previous order, and does not affect the main Dictionary or Spanish.

The derived score data is distributed under [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). [wordfreq and SUBTLEX attribution](licenses/wordfreq-data-attribution.txt) is bundled as a Windows app asset; wordfreq uses freely available SUBTLEX frequency data created by Marc Brysbaert et al. To regenerate, obtain `wordfreq/data/large_en.msgpack.gz` from the pinned commit (SHA-256 `dffae8066b78dce0a6667cf5f58e567054f902674667090a7ac8a8a44628b05c`), install Python `msgpack`, and run `python tool/generate_english_frequency.py <path-to-large_en.msgpack.gz>` after updating the English word files. The generator verifies the data hash.
