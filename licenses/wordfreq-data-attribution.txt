English frequency estimates are derived from wordfreq's English large list
at commit 912caf64b657478d1dff1138efdc078947d54bb1, by Robyn Speer:
https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq

The generated English frequency score table is derived from wordfreq data,
available under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
No text from CipherBench's bundled quotes was used to calculate the scores.

wordfreq incorporates SUBTLEX subtitle frequency data, created by Marc
Brysbaert et al. SUBTLEX is freely available at:
https://crr.ugent.be/programs-data/subtitle-frequencies
The full source attribution and licensing notes are at:
https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq/blob/master/NOTICE.md
